# A visit time with 6 households is full

> Six different households book the last listed visit time; a seventh household finds it marked Full and cannot book it.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0161 in "Phone number", choose the last option listed in "Visit time", leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit".

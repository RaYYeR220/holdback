# One-person household $1 over the weekly limit is not eligible

> A household of 1 with no benefits and $569 weekly income is $1 above the published weekly limit of $568, so it is not eligible.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 569 and "How often is this income received?" to "Weekly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19139, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Not eligible." and "Limit applied: $568 per week for a household of 1."

# Household in ZIP 19103 is outside the service area

> A household in ZIP 19103, which we do not serve, is told it is outside our service area and gets no eligibility result, even though its income is under the limit.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19103, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Outside our service area." and shows neither "Eligible." nor "Not eligible."

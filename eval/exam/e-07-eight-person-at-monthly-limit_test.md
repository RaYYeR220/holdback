# Eight-person household at the $8,591 monthly limit is eligible

> A household of 8 with no benefits and $8,591 monthly income is exactly at the published monthly limit, so it is eligible.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 8, "Household income ($)" to 8591 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Eligible." and "Limit applied: $8,591 per month for a household of 8." and does not say "Not eligible."

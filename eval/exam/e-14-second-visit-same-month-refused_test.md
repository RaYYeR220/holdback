# Second visit in the same calendar month is refused

> A household that has booked the first listed visit time cannot also book the second listed visit time, which is in the same calendar month.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0114 in "Phone number", choose the first option listed in "Visit time", leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit".

## Step 2

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0114 in "Phone number", choose the second option listed in "Visit time", leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit"; then assert the page says "This household already has a visit booked in that month." and does not say "Visit booked."

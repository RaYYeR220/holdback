# Ten-person household at the $124,098 yearly limit is eligible

> A household of 10 has an annual limit of $103,082 plus two $10,508 increments, $124,098; with no benefits and $124,098 yearly income it is eligible.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 10, "Household income ($)" to 124098 and "How often is this income received?" to "Yearly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19143, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Eligible." and "Limit applied: $124,098 per year for a household of 10."

# SNAP household outside the service area gets no eligibility result

> A household in ZIP 19102 in which someone receives SNAP is still outside our service area: the ZIP code is checked before benefits.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 500 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", tick "SNAP", leave "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19102, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Outside our service area." and shows neither "Eligible." nor "Not eligible."

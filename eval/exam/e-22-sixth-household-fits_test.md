# The sixth household still gets a visit time

> Five different households book the second-latest visit time listed; a sixth household can still book it, because a visit time takes up to 6 households.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0221 in "Phone number", choose the second-latest Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday visit time listed in "Visit time", leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit".

# Visit in the next calendar month is allowed

> A household that has booked the first listed visit time can still book a visit time in the following calendar month.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0115 in "Phone number", choose the first option listed in "Visit time", leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit".

## Step 2

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0115 in "Phone number", in "Visit time" choose the earliest option whose date is in the calendar month after the month of the first option listed, leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit"; then assert the page says "Visit booked."

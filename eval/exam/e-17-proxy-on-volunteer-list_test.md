# Proxy name appears on the volunteer list

> A household that names Dana Whitfield as its proxy when booking today's first visit time shows that proxy on today's volunteer list.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0117 in "Phone number", choose the first option listed in "Visit time", enter Dana Whitfield in "Proxy name (optional)", and choose "Book visit".

## Step 2

Choose the "Volunteer list" link at the top of the page; then assert that under "Expected today" the visit with phone number 215-555-0117 shows Dana Whitfield in the "Proxy" column.

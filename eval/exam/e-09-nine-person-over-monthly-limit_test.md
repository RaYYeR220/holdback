# Nine-person household $1 over the monthly limit is not eligible

> A household of 9 with no benefits and $9,468 monthly income is $1 above its monthly limit of $9,467, so it is not eligible.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 9, "Household income ($)" to 9468 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19139, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Not eligible." and "Limit applied: $9,467 per month for a household of 9."

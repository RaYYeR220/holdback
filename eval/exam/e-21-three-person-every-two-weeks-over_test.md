# Three-person household paid $1,944 every two weeks is not eligible

> A household of 3 with no benefits paid $1,944 every two weeks counts $50,544 a year, above the annual limit of $50,542, so it is not eligible.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 3, "Household income ($)" to 1944 and "How often is this income received?" to "Every two weeks", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19143, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Not eligible." and "Limit applied: $50,542 per year for a household of 3."

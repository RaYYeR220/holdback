# Mark picked up moves the visit to Picked up

> After a volunteer marks today's visit of a household as picked up, the visit is listed under Picked up instead of Expected today, and stays there after a reload.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, on the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 1, "Household income ($)" to 1000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", leave "SNAP", "TANF", "SSI" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then choose "Book a visit", and on the "Book a visit" page enter 215-555-0118 in "Phone number", choose the earliest Tuesday, Thursday or Saturday visit time listed in "Visit time", leave "Proxy name (optional)" empty, and choose "Book visit".

## Step 2

Choose the "Volunteer list" link at the top of the page, and under "Expected today" choose "Mark picked up" on the visit with phone number 215-555-0118.

## Step 3

Reload the page; then assert the visit with phone number 215-555-0118 is listed under "Picked up" and is no longer listed under "Expected today".

# Household receiving SSI is eligible regardless of income

> A household of 2 in which someone receives SSI is eligible even with $9,000 monthly income, far above its income limit.

## Step 1

At {{start_url}}, open the "Food pantry eligibility check" page.

## Step 2

On the "Food pantry eligibility check" page, set "Household size" to 2, "Household income ($)" to 9000 and "How often is this income received?" to "Monthly", tick "SSI", leave "SNAP", "TANF" and "Medicaid" unticked, set "ZIP code" to 19104, and choose "Check eligibility"; then assert the result below the button says "Eligible." and "Reason: someone in the household receives SNAP, TANF, SSI or Medicaid."
